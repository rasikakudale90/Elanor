# Élanor — Overall Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview

**Élanor** is a premium skincare e-commerce platform designed to make skincare shopping more personalized, informative, and enjoyable.

The platform combines a luxury shopping experience with helpful skincare discovery tools. Customers can explore products, understand ingredients and skin concerns, build a personalized skincare routine, compare products, save products, manage their cart, and complete a purchase through a simple and visually refined experience.

The product should feel like a **premium beauty destination**, not a generic online store.

---

## 2. Product Vision

Élanor aims to make skincare shopping easier by bringing together:

- Premium product discovery
- Skin-concern-based shopping
- Ingredient education
- Personalized routine recommendations
- Product comparison
- Simple and convenient shopping
- A visually premium brand experience

The customer should be able to move naturally from:

**Discover → Understand → Choose → Save → Purchase**

---

## 3. Product Goals

### Primary Goals

1. Create a premium skincare shopping experience.
2. Help customers discover products based on their needs and concerns.
3. Make skincare ingredients easier to understand.
4. Help customers create a suitable skincare routine.
5. Make comparing and choosing products easier.
6. Provide a smooth shopping journey from product discovery to checkout.
7. Build trust through clear product information and helpful content.
8. Maintain a consistent luxury brand identity across the entire experience.

### Secondary Goals

- Encourage product discovery.
- Increase customer confidence before purchase.
- Make returning customers' shopping experience easier.
- Create opportunities for customers to explore brands, ingredients, and skincare concerns.

---

## 4. Target Customers

Élanor is intended for people who:

- Shop for skincare products online.
- Want to discover products based on specific skin concerns.
- Want to understand skincare ingredients before buying.
- Prefer curated or personalized recommendations.
- Like premium and visually polished shopping experiences.
- Want an easier way to compare skincare products.
- Need help building or improving a skincare routine.

The experience should remain simple enough for someone who is not highly knowledgeable about skincare.

---

## 5. Core Customer Experience

A typical customer journey should look like:

### Step 1 — Discover

The customer enters Élanor and sees curated skincare products, collections, concerns, brands, and editorial content.

### Step 2 — Explore

The customer can browse the store, search for products, explore categories, or shop according to a skin concern.

### Step 3 — Understand

The customer can view detailed product information and learn about ingredients, benefits, usage, reviews, and other relevant information.

### Step 4 — Decide

The customer can compare products, save products to a wishlist, or use the AI Routine Builder for personalized guidance.

### Step 5 — Purchase

The customer adds products to the cart, reviews the order, provides delivery information, selects the available checkout option, and completes the purchase journey.

### Step 6 — Return

Customers can continue discovering products, revisit saved products, explore recently viewed items, and use Élanor's discovery tools again.

---

# 6. Main Product Areas

## 6.1 Home

The homepage should introduce the Élanor brand and guide customers toward product discovery.

It should include:

- Premium hero section
- Featured collections
- Best sellers
- New arrivals
- Shop by concern
- Editorial or educational content
- Ingredient spotlight
- Featured products
- Trust-building content
- Newsletter section
- Footer navigation

The homepage should communicate the brand immediately while also making shopping easy.

---

## 6.2 Product Discovery / Shop

Customers should be able to browse the complete product collection.

The shopping experience should support:

- Product categories
- Skin concerns
- Brands
- Price ranges
- Ratings
- Availability
- Product sorting
- Active filters
- Product search
- Product cards
- Quick product actions

Customers should be able to quickly narrow down the products that are relevant to them.

---

## 6.3 Shop by Concern

Customers should be able to discover products according to skincare concerns.

Examples of concerns may include:

- Acne and breakouts
- Dryness
- Dehydration
- Uneven tone
- Dullness
- Texture
- Sensitivity
- Other supported skincare concerns

Each concern should help customers discover relevant products and educational information.

---

## 6.4 Product Details

Every product should have a detailed product page.

The product experience should provide:

- Product images
- Product name
- Brand
- Price
- Product variants where applicable
- Product benefits
- Ingredients
- How to use
- Product information
- Reviews
- Frequently asked questions
- Related products
- Recently viewed products
- Add-to-cart action
- Wishlist action

The product page should provide enough information for customers to make an informed shopping decision without feeling overwhelming.

---

# 7. AI Routine Builder

The AI Routine Builder is one of Élanor's key differentiating experiences.

It should help customers discover products that may fit their skincare needs.

The experience should:

1. Ask simple questions.
2. Understand the customer's skincare preferences and concerns.
3. Process the provided information.
4. Present a personalized routine.
5. Recommend relevant products.
6. Explain why each recommended product is included.
7. Allow customers to explore or purchase recommended products.

The experience should feel like a natural part of Élanor's shopping journey rather than a separate generic chatbot.

For the initial product, the recommendations can be powered by prepared/mock responses until the actual intelligent recommendation system is connected.

---

# 8. Ingredient Explorer

The Ingredient Explorer should help customers understand skincare ingredients.

Customers should be able to:

- Search for ingredients.
- Explore ingredient information.
- Understand their commonly associated benefits.
- Discover products containing an ingredient.
- Move from ingredient information directly to relevant products.

The feature should make ingredient education approachable and easy to understand.

---

# 9. Product Compare

Customers should be able to compare selected skincare products.

Comparison should make it easier to understand differences such as:

- Price
- Product type
- Key ingredients
- Benefits
- Skin concerns
- Product features
- Other relevant product information

The purpose is to help customers understand their options without forcing them to open multiple product pages repeatedly.

---

# 10. Search

Élanor should provide a convenient product discovery search experience.

Search should support:

- Product search
- Brand search
- Ingredient search
- Relevant suggestions
- Recent searches
- Popular searches
- Empty search results
- Clear search states

Search should be quick and easy to access from the main navigation.

---

# 11. Brands

Customers should be able to explore skincare brands available on Élanor.

The brand experience should allow customers to:

- Discover brands.
- View products from a brand.
- Explore brand information where available.
- Move from a brand directly into its product collection.

---

# 12. Wishlist

Customers should be able to save products they are interested in.

Wishlist functionality should include:

- Add product to wishlist.
- Remove product from wishlist.
- View saved products.
- Move a saved product to cart.
- Empty wishlist experience.
- Easy access from the main shopping experience.

The wishlist should help customers save products for later without interrupting their browsing.

---

# 13. Cart

The cart should provide a clear summary of selected products.

Customers should be able to:

- View products.
- Increase or decrease quantities.
- Remove products.
- Review prices.
- See applicable totals.
- Enter or view coupon information where supported.
- See shipping information where supported.
- Continue to checkout.

The cart should be accessible without forcing customers to leave their current shopping journey.

---

# 14. Checkout

The checkout should provide a simple and clear purchasing journey.

The intended flow is:

**Cart → Address → Shipping → Order Review → Payment → Success**

The checkout experience should include:

- Customer delivery information
- Shipping selection where applicable
- Order summary
- Product details
- Pricing summary
- Payment step/placeholders
- Order confirmation
- Clear success state

The initial frontend product should focus on the complete customer experience. Actual payment processing and backend order processing can be connected later.

---

# 15. Navigation

Élanor should provide consistent navigation across the product.

The navigation experience should include:

- Élanor branding
- Main shopping navigation
- Product discovery
- Search
- Wishlist
- Cart
- Brand/category access
- Mobile navigation

On smaller screens, the navigation should adapt to the available space while keeping the important actions easy to reach.

---

# 16. Mobile Experience

Élanor should provide a complete experience across:

- Desktop
- Tablet
- Mobile

The mobile experience should not simply shrink the desktop layout.

Interactions should be adapted where necessary, for example:

- Navigation becomes mobile-friendly.
- Filters can open as a mobile panel.
- Hover-based interactions become touch-friendly.
- Multi-column layouts become easier-to-scan single-column layouts.
- Important actions remain easy to reach.

---

# 17. Visual & Brand Experience

Élanor should consistently communicate:

- Luxury
- Elegance
- Trust
- Calmness
- Modern skincare
- Premium quality
- Editorial beauty

The existing prototype is the **visual reference for the final product**.

The final experience should preserve the prototype's:

- Overall layout
- Typography
- Colors
- Spacing
- Product presentation
- Visual hierarchy
- Imagery
- Cards
- Navigation style
- Responsive behavior
- Interactions
- Brand atmosphere

The goal is not to redesign the product during implementation.

---

# 18. Motion & Interaction Experience

Motion should make Élanor feel refined and premium without distracting customers.

Animations should be:

- Smooth
- Subtle
- Purposeful
- Fast enough to keep shopping comfortable
- Consistent with the brand

Motion may be used for:

- Hero sections
- Product reveals
- Product image changes
- Page sections
- Navigation
- Search
- Product galleries
- Cart interactions
- Modals and drawers
- Important calls to action

Animations should never make customers wait unnecessarily before accessing products or purchasing.

Reduced-motion preferences should be respected.

---

# 19. Trust & Product Information

Élanor should provide customers with enough information to shop confidently.

Important information should be presented clearly, including:

- Product benefits
- Ingredients
- Usage
- Product details
- Reviews
- Frequently asked questions
- Brand information where available
- Pricing
- Availability

Information should be organized so customers can understand products without excessive effort.

---

# 20. Empty, Loading & Error Experiences

Every major customer journey should have appropriate states.

This includes:

- Loading states
- Empty search results
- Empty wishlist
- Empty cart
- Product not found
- General error states
- Unavailable product states
- Checkout error states

These states should still feel consistent with the Élanor brand.

---

# 21. Content & Product Data

The product should initially work with realistic product information for the frontend experience.

Product information should be structured consistently so that the same customer experience can later use real product information from the backend.

The initial experience should not depend on manually rebuilding pages when real product data becomes available.

---

# 22. Future Backend Integration

The initial project focuses on the customer-facing frontend.

The future system is expected to connect the experience with backend services for areas such as:

- Products
- Categories
- Brands
- Inventory
- Wishlist
- Cart
- Orders
- Customer information
- Reviews
- Search
- AI recommendations

The transition from the initial frontend experience to the future backend should preserve the customer-facing experience wherever possible.

---

# 23. Initial Product Scope

## Included

- Homepage
- Shop
- Product listing and filtering
- Product details
- Shop by concern
- Brands
- Search
- Ingredient Explorer
- Product Compare
- AI Routine Builder
- Wishlist
- Cart
- Checkout experience
- Responsive experience
- Premium visual experience
- Product discovery and educational content
- Loading, empty, error, and success states

## Not Included in the Initial Frontend Scope

- Backend development
- Database development
- Real payment processing
- Authentication system
- Admin dashboard
- CMS
- Real-time inventory system
- Full production AI infrastructure
- Other future-scope functionality unless explicitly approved

These can be introduced in later phases.

---

# 24. Product Principles

Every feature should follow these principles:

### 1. Customer First

The experience should make shopping easier, not more complicated.

### 2. Premium but Simple

Élanor should feel luxurious without becoming difficult to use.

### 3. Helpful, Not Overwhelming

Information and recommendations should assist customers without creating unnecessary complexity.

### 4. Consistent

Every part of the product should feel like the same Élanor brand.

### 5. Purposeful Innovation

Features such as AI Routine Builder and Ingredient Explorer should solve real customer problems.

### 6. Mobile Friendly

Customers should receive a complete experience regardless of device.

### 7. Trust Through Clarity

Product information, pricing, and shopping actions should remain clear.

### 8. Prototype as the Visual Contract

The existing prototype defines the intended visual experience unless a change is explicitly requested.

---

# 25. Success Criteria

The Élanor product should be considered successful when customers can:

- Discover skincare products easily.
- Browse products by category and concern.
- Search for products and ingredients.
- Understand product ingredients and benefits.
- Compare products.
- Receive a personalized skincare routine.
- Save products for later.
- Add products to their cart.
- Complete the checkout journey.
- Use the experience comfortably on desktop and mobile.
- Understand what each product offers before purchasing.
- Experience a consistent premium Élanor brand throughout the journey.

Most importantly, the platform should make skincare shopping feel **personal, clear, premium, and enjoyable**.

---

# 26. Final Product Statement

**Élanor is a premium skincare e-commerce experience that combines product discovery, skincare education, personalization, and convenient shopping in one elegant platform.**

The product should help customers move from **“What should I use?”** to **“I understand what I need and I know what to choose.”**

The final experience should preserve the identity and quality established by the existing Élanor prototype while providing a strong foundation for future expansion.
